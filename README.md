\# AI Study Planner Agent



An Agentic AI study planning application that creates personalized day-wise study plans based on subjects, topics, available study hours, duration, difficulty level, and student requirements.



\## Problem Statement



Students often struggle to organize multiple subjects and topics within limited time. A fixed timetable may not match their available study hours, difficulty level, or exam requirements.



The goal of this project is to create an AI-powered study planning agent that can understand these constraints and generate a practical, personalized study plan.



\## Solution



AI Study Planner Agent takes the student's study requirements and uses a Groq-powered LLM to generate a structured study plan.



The generated plan includes:



\- Day-wise study schedule

\- Topic-based tasks

\- Practice activities

\- Revision planning

\- Study tips

\- Task checklist

\- Progress tracking

\- Plan regeneration when requirements change

\- Downloadable study plan



\## Key Features



\### Personalized Planning



The user can provide:



\- Subjects

\- Topics

\- Study duration

\- Available study hours per day

\- Overall difficulty

\- Additional requirements



\### AI-Based Planning



The AI analyzes the provided subjects, topics, time constraints, and requirements before generating the study plan.



\### Multiple Subjects



Students can add multiple subjects with different topics.



\### Task Checklist



Generated study tasks can be marked as completed.



\### Progress Tracking



The application tracks completed tasks and shows study progress.



\### Plan Regeneration



Students can generate a new plan when their availability or requirements change.



\### Download



The generated study plan can be downloaded for later use.



\## Agentic AI Workflow



The application follows a simple agentic workflow:



1\. Student enters study goals.

2\. Student provides study constraints such as duration, available hours, and difficulty.

3\. AI Planning Agent analyzes the requirements.

4\. Topics are prioritized based on difficulty and requirements.

5\. Available study time is allocated across topics.

6\. The AI generates day-wise study tasks.

7\. Student completes tasks and tracks progress.

8\. If requirements change, the plan can be regenerated.
